# Zhiling Upload Verification

Turn a project objective into an executable plan.

1. Clarify the objective, scope, constraints, and deadline.
2. Split the objective into measurable milestones.
3. Assign one owner and one acceptance criterion to every milestone.
4. Record dependencies, risks, and fallback actions.
5. Return a concise table ordered by execution sequence.

Do not invent owners, dates, or business constraints. Mark missing information as pending confirmation.
