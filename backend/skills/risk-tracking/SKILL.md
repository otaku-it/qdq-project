# Risk Tracking

Maintain a concise and actionable risk register.

1. Capture each risk as a possible future event.
2. Assess probability and impact with an explicit scale.
3. Add an owner, mitigation, trigger, and contingency when known.
4. Separate active issues from risks.
5. Sort by exposure and call out overdue mitigation actions.
